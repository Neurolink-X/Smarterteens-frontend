# India-first learning priorities
## Evidence and scope
- ASER 2024: rural learning outcomes and digital skills, not a representative claim about every Indian school or teenager: https://asercentre.org/aser-2024/
- Economic Survey 2025–26: education-to-skilling alignment: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2219936&lang=1&reg=1
- RBI: public financial literacy: https://www.rbi.org.in/FinancialEducation/fame.aspx
- WEF Future of Jobs 2025: employer survey expectations, not a guarantee of jobs: https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025
## P0: learning foundations and adoption
Initial 16+ audience (Class 11–12); remedial mathematics, comprehension, communication, financial literacy, AI and digital verification. Free lightweight browser-based tasks, English/Hindi/Hinglish. Each activity: context → choice → reasoning feedback → project → related course. Never claim curriculum alignment, mastery or certification based on a single practice check.
## P1: application and industry exposure
Original virtual exercises across robotics, software, data, cloud, semiconductor basics, climate, energy, space, biotechnology and quantum concepts. Future World distinguishes dated evidence from hypothetical 2030–2050 scenarios. No hazardous physical experiments or real financial transactions.
## Next delivery sequence
1. Expand from isolated short tasks to progressive concept/practice/application sequences, class and subject mapping reviewed by educators.
2. Build misconception-level adaptive practice and spaced review; use item-level evidence, not personality or aptitude labels.
3. Add safe school/industry projects, evidence rubric, educator review, bilingual accessibility and low-bandwidth usability.
4. Add authenticated cloud progress, verified portfolio and consented guardian/teacher access only after real role isolation, privacy and staging checks. Current skills checks are local.
5. Add course marketplace and coaching revenue features only once operations and permissions are verified.
## Acceptance criteria
Measure first-attempt and repeat performance on comparable new items, quality of project explanations, accessible mobile completion and voluntary continued learning. No invented outcome metrics; teacher content review and actual user research are required. Follow the common quality gate for every code change.
