# Manual UPI collection (no payment gateway)
The original user-provided QR was decoded and a lossless QR generated from the exact payload; both recipient and scan payload were verified locally. Store the QR image and recipient in production-only encrypted ST_UPI_CONFIG, not public source or test fixtures. ST_UPI_ENABLED must be explicitly true after bank account suitability, final billing/tax documentation, reviewer provisioning and deployed smoke checks. Default disabled. Owner sees instructions only after an approved quote and reviewer final amount authorization. Static QR requires the payer to enter the exact amount. Reference submission is an unverified claim. Reviewer must independently reconcile actual bank credit and may reject a claim. Unique verified RRN and payment constraints prevent duplicate recognition. No automatic subscription activation, gateway, real payments in CI or accounting revenue recognition. Do not request PIN, OTP, screenshots or bank credentials. Production account changes require new reviewed instructions. Current pilot has no refund or dispute automation; handle discrepancies manually before accepting live payments.
