# SmarterTeens: practical education and Coaching OS strategy
## Revenue and market
Coaching OS is the primary recurring-revenue hypothesis. Practical learning academy and institute SaaS are very high priorities; school partnerships and verified skill portfolios are high. Traditional coaching, owned physical chains and chatbot-only education are low priorities initially. Pilot with existing local institutes; 50–500 students per institute is a planning range, not a measured acquisition result. Pricing is indicative until willingness to pay, onboarding cost and retention are measured.
P0: class 11–12 (16+), local coaching owners and parents. P1: class 6–10 and private schools via age-appropriate compliant partnerships. P2: college learners and industry skills. Competition preparation: discovery and reviewed practice materials; no rank guarantees or unreviewed exam alignment.
## Learning approach
Adapt the user-proposed international approaches, not national stereotypes or claims of uniform teaching: interdisciplinary projects (Finland inspiration), mathematics concept clarity (Singapore), reflection and teamwork (Japan), vocational tasks (Germany), project-based entrepreneurship (USA), affordable multilingual learning (India). Each activity maps to prerequisites, concept, example, safe simulation, reasoning feedback and optional project evidence.
Future World 2030–2050 distinguishes dated primary-source evidence from illustrative scenarios. Future predictions require source, date, assumptions and uncertainty. No extrapolation masquerading as a verified development.
## Coaching OS delivery
Owner pilot: institute, admissions, batches, attendance, invoices and offline receipts. Question-bank paper draft is rule-based, not AI. Branded websites, staff accounts, parent-linked access, real student records, marketplace payments, teacher-verified portfolios, SaaS subscriptions and AI question generation require dedicated database, secure tenant isolation, review and E2E. Do not sell unavailable features.
## Acceptance measures
Track actual practice improvement on comparable questions (not just clicks), completed projects, educator-reviewed quality, institute onboarding completion, active paying institutes, renewal and support workload. No fabricated metrics; define baselines before interpreting impact.
## Architecture
Next.js App Router and incremental TypeScript on Node/Vercel; Supabase/Postgres with RLS and explicit memberships. Existing Java repo remains a reference, not a deployment dependency. No paid services enabled without approval.
