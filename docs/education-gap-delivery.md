# Education-gap delivery map
This is a product implementation map, not a claim of measured educational outcomes.
| Gap | Implemented now | Not yet delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Rote learning | Concept lessons, worked examples, explanations and foundational numeracy | Full curriculum alignment and independent educator review |
| Theory/practice | Project briefs and interactive labs | Teacher-reviewed submitted projects |
| One-size-fits-all | Eight-item practice results mapped to related courses; chosen-interest roadmap | Validated adaptive assessment, longitudinal mastery model |
| Career awareness | Five user-selected exploration paths, practical activities | Verified career information and mentor network |
| Language | English, Hindi and Hinglish practice check | Full course and platform localization |
| Financial literacy | Business cash/profit simulator and budget scenarios in practice | Broader personal finance curriculum |
| AI literacy | Source verification, privacy and guided practice | AI tutor/provider integration and safety evaluation |
| Parent visibility | Student-controlled printable device progress | Verified parent accounts, consent and server-side evidence |
| Coaching management | Existing restricted owner pilot, batches, attendance and offline fee ledger | Provisioned dedicated database, staff roles and live E2E |
| Affordability | Free self-paced mini courses and labs | Verified local partners and sustainable pricing |
Do not claim national test statistics or skill-change forecasts as platform outcomes. Before public marketing, verify the original ASER 2024 and WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 sources. No invented learner counts, certifications, or aptitude predictions.
